## 练习 D：Mock 多轮

补全代码，使第二轮回复为「第二句」：

```python
R1 = {..., "choices": [{"message": {"role": "assistant", "content": "第一句"}, ...}]}
R2 = {..., "choices": [{"message": {"role": "assistant", "content": "第二句"}, ...}]}
client = make_mock_client([R1, R2])
a = ChatAssistant(client=client)
outs = a.run_scripted(["问题1", "问题2"])
assert any("第二句" in o for o in outs)
```
